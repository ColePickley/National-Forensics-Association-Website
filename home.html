NFA Code of Standards & Event Rules

Intercollegiate Competition Divisions

Individual Events (IE)

Public Address, Interpretation & Limited Prep

No speech or program used in competition may have been used prior to September 1 preceding the National Championship Tournament, and no student may perform two cuttings from the same literary work in the same year.

Extemporaneous Speaking: Speeches must be the original work of the speaker prepared after topics are drawn; consultation after draw is strictly prohibited.

One-Person Policy Debate

NFA Lincoln-Douglas Debate

Format & Resolution: One-person policy debate emphasizing ethical advocacy, rigorous research, and persuasive public communication on an annual national policy topic.

Communication Emphasis: Rapid-fire "spread delivery" is antithetical to the event. Speeches must remain comprehensible and persuasive, with accurate, contextualized evidence. The annual resolution is announced every July 1.

Eligibility & Qualification

National Championship Tournament Eligibility

Contestants must be undergraduate students enrolled at the institution they represent for more than a course in forensics, within eight semesters of competitive eligibility (and up to four years of national tournament competition). The tournament qualification and entry deadline falls on the third Monday prior to the National Championship Tournament.

About the National Forensic Journal

The National Forensic Journal welcomes double-spaced APA (7th ed.) manuscripts addressing instruction, practice, critique, and research in speech, debate, and performance activities. Submissions undergo anonymous peer review (~3 months) and require a 250-word abstract.

Editorial Leadership: Dr. Justin Rudnick (Editor) & Dr. Kevin Minch (Associate Editor). Questions: nfajournal@gmail.com.

NFA Research Committee

Conducting Research at Nationals

All research conducted at the NFA National Tournament must be approved in advance by the NFA Research Committee and must not disrupt students or the tournament schedule:

  • 1-Page Topic + 1-Page Methodology Due February 10
  • Committee Approval / Denial Notification February 28
  • Official Institutional IRB Approval Due April 1
  • Preliminary Findings Report Due September 30
