Registration & Qualifications

Coaches Registration Guide

All entries must be submitted through Speechwire by March 30, 2026 at 6 pm CST. No entries will be accepted after this deadline.

  • All qualifications will be verified through Speechwire. Tournaments not hosted on Speechwire must be reported manually.
  • Manual reports should include the entire tournament results, not just the qualifications you plan to use.
  • In-Person & Speechwire E-Ballots: The tournament is fully in-person at Tennessee State University, but e-ballots will be run via Speechwire. Coaches must provide an accurate email address for every student and judge.
  • School names, student names, and judge names will appear exactly as entered on all tournament materials. Add student name pronunciations and pronouns in Speechwire.
  • Enrollment Verification: All teams must provide an Enrollment Verification form from their Registrar. Scan and email a copy to nfatournamentcommittee@gmail.com when registering on Speechwire, and bring the original to Registration.

E-Ballots & Tech Support

Electronic Ballots Guidelines

  • Create a ballot account at live.speechwire.com using your valid email address.
  • Bring a fully charged device to judge on.
  • Click “Start” when you arrive in your room so tab knows you are present.
  • Return ranks immediately after the round; comments can be revised until the tournament concludes.
  • Experiencing tech problems or dead batteries? Take notes on paper, then report to the tab room to enter ranks via computer lab.
