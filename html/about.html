History

In 1971, Dr. Seth Hawkins, concluding that the needs of the forensics community warranted a National I.E. Tournament, invented a national championship in individual events, declared it official by fiat and sent invitations. The tournament featured Prose, Poetry, Extemporaneous, Impromptu, After-Dinner Speaking and Oratory.

The following 23 schools were in attendance: American University (DC), Ball State University (IN), Defiance College (OH), Eastern Kentucky University, Eastern Michigan University, Evangel College (MO), Georgetown College (KY), Heidelberg College (OH), University of Kentucky, Lehigh University (PA), University of Southern Maine, University of Maryland, Miami University (OH), Niagara University (NY), Ohio University, Ohio Northern University, Purdue University (IN), Shepherd University (WV), Southern Connecticut State University, St John’s College (NY), St. Rose College (NY), West Chester University (PA) and Wright State University (OH).

In 1973, a draft constitution for the National Forensic Association was written in January to create a governing body for the I.E. Tournament. The constitution was accepted at the tournament in April at Eastern Michigan University.

Standing Committees

Committee Description
Awards The Awards Committee oversees the selection of professional awards such as the Eddie Myers Distinguished Service Award, manages the cost and ordering of the National Championship Awards, and evaluates and implements award changes for the Championship Tournament.
Constitution The Constitution Committee monitors the NFA Constitution and Bylaws. It advises the NFA Executive Council and membership on constitutional and bylaw concerns as they emerge. It regularly audits the governing documents to determine whether current organisational practice is consistent with them and proposes amendments when necessary. It also helps NFA members draft general proposals and amendments to the Constitution and Bylaws in the context of the current governing documents.
Elections This committee ensures that a full slate of qualified candidates runs for each office and verifies elections and ballot results.
Ethics This committee oversees the Code of Standards and the Code of Ethics, which the NFA National Council and NFA Tournament Committee enforce. When necessary, the committee considers and proposes changes to both codes.
Equity, Diversity and Inclusivity This committee monitors and promotes NFA’s policies on equity, diversity, and inclusivity. It advises NFA on concerns that members raise about bias and accessibility. The committee has created documents to help coaches and students navigate issues of access.
Extemporaneous This committee creates the extemporaneous topics for the National Championship Tournament.
Hall of Fame This committee oversees nominations and selection for Hall of Fame membership, as well as publicity and promotion of the Hall.
Impromptu This committee creates the impromptu topics for the National Championship Tournament.
Research This committee oversees all research conducted at the National Championship Tournament.
Social Media This committee maintains an NFA presence on social media channels.
Tournament The Constitution names three members of this committee. The Tournament Director serves as chair, and the President and the Vice President for Administration serve alongside the chair.
Lincoln-Douglas Committee members are elected from the NFA membership to oversee NFA Lincoln-Douglas Debate.

Awards

The National Forensic Association sponsors two prestigious awards: the Eddie Myers Distinguished Service Award and the Hall of Fame Award.

Eddie Myers Distinguished Service

The Eddie Myers NFA Service Award is named in memory of Dr. Louis Edward Myers, NFA Tournament Director from 1997 to 2003. Nationally prominent as a forensics coach, he was awarded the NFA Distinguished Service Award by the NFA in 2003 and the NFA President’s Award for dedication in Service in 2002.

DEADLINE FOR SUBMISSIONS: The deadline for the Eddie Myers award is March 15, 2026.

Eddie Myers NFA Distinguished Service Award Nominations must be sent to the Chair of the Awards committee, currently Megan Koch.

Hall Of Fame

The Hall of Fame honors those students that made an impact while competing in forensics and continued to make an impact after leaving the activity. Areas to consider are: forensics competitive excellence, forensics community citizenship, and forensics ambassadorship.

DEADLINE FOR SUBMISSIONS: The nomination deadline for the NFA Hall of Fame is January 15, 2026.

NFA Hall of Fame Nominations must be sent to the Chair of the Hall of Fame Committee, currently Jennifer Talbert.

Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion

The NFA embraces human diversity as a core value. We express this value best in the inclusion and affirmation of the various identity groups to which our members and participants belong. This commitment to inclusiveness embraces identities and social roles that arise from but are not limited to race, color, ethnicity, national origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, religion, or ability status. We affirm and uphold the right to basic human dignity and respect across these diverse forms of human expression.
