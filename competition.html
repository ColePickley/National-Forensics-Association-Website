Public Address, Interpretation & Limited Prep

No speech or program used in competition may have been used prior to September 1 preceding the National Championship Tournament, and no student may perform two cuttings from the same literary work in the same year.

Extemporaneous Speaking: Speeches must be the original work of the speaker prepared after topics are drawn; consultation after draw is strictly prohibited.

One-on-One Policy Debate

NFA Lincoln-Douglas Debate

NFA-LD on the intercollegiate level is a one-person, persuasive, policy-oriented debate evaluated on traditional stock issues: Harms, Inherency, Solvency, and Topicality.

Communication Emphasis: Rapid-fire "spread delivery" is antithetical to the event. Speeches must remain comprehensible and persuasive, with accurate, contextualized evidence. The annual resolution is announced every July 1.

Official LD Speech Time Limits

  • 1st Affirmative Constructive: 6 minutes (followed by 3 min Cross-Ex)
  • 1st Negative Constructive: 7 minutes (followed by 3 min Cross-Ex)
  • 1st Affirmative Rebuttal: 6 minutes
  • Negative Rebuttal: 6 minutes
  • 2nd Affirmative Rebuttal: 3 minutes
  • Preparation Time: 4 minutes per debater

Innovation in Forensics

Experimental Events

Proposals for an experimental event at the National Tournament must be submitted to the National Council by April 1 and include the event name, rules, rationale, and authors' names and affiliations. The National Council selects proposals for the NFA Student Assembly, which may choose up to one (1) event for implementation at the following year's National Tournament.

Note: Experimental events do not count toward sweepstakes or pentathlon, are contingent upon available space, and cannot become regular NFA events unless they constitutionally replace an existing event.

Eligibility & Qualification

National Championship Tournament Eligibility

Contestants must be undergraduate students enrolled at the institution they represent for more than a course in forensics, within eight semesters of competitive eligibility (and up to four years of national tournament competition). The tournament qualification and entry deadline falls on the third Monday prior to the National Championship Tournament.
